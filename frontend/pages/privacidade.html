Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-v1 · ARBITRAJUS · GDPR & US Data Privacy Compliant (including CCPA)

1. Who we are

ARBITRAJUS is a digital platform for conciliation, mediation and arbitration. Data controller: ARBITRAJUS. Privacy contact: contact@arbitrajus.com.

2. Data we process

  • Accreditation: name, email, phone, practice area, city, education and experience provided voluntarily.
  • Professional outreach: professional email and minimal B2B contact data, when obtained from a legitimate public source or referral.
  • Platform: case data, documents and authentication data, as needed to provide the services.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Accreditation review: consent.
  • Invitations to the professional panel: legitimate interest, with opt-out at any time.
  • Case administration: contract performance and legal obligations.

We design our practices to be GDPR & US Data Privacy Compliant, including strong state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

4. Your rights

You may, at any time:

  • Confirm whether we process your data and access it
  • Correct incomplete or outdated data
  • Unsubscribe from email invitations
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Withdraw consent

Use the Data deletion / unsubscribe page or email contact@arbitrajus.com.

5. Retention and automatic deletion

Prospects who do not start accreditation and do not engage for 60 days may have data deleted automatically. Deletion requests are handled within 15 business days, subject to legal retention duties.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with infrastructure providers (hosting, transactional email) under contract and only to operate the platform.

7. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss or alteration.
